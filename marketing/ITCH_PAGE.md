## Page text
**You can't walk. Every move is a blast that pushes you the opposite way.**

Do You Got Skill is a precision platformer where momentum is everything. Point where you want to
go, fire, and let gravity do the rest. Chain shots, grab swings, phase through walls and ride
elevators across neon-lit levels that punish sloppy aim and reward clean lines.

**This is the free demo: worlds 1-3 (15 levels).** The full game has 60 levels across 10 worlds,
including 10 three-screen finals.

**Features**
- Five difficulties, including God Mode: one life for the whole run
- Beat your best time AND your fewest shots, then race the ghost of your best run
- Stars for flawless clears, plus ball colours you unlock as you clear levels
- Practice mode with infinite shots
- Rebindable keys, reduced-flashing mode and high-contrast hazards

**Controls:** point with the mouse, click or Space to fire, R retry, Esc pause. (Rebindable in Settings.)

**Feedback wanted!** There's a "Send feedback" button in the menu. Tell me which level made you quit.
