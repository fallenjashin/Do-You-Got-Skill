## Long description
**You can't walk. You can only fire.**

Do You Got Skill is a precision platformer with one rule: every shot pushes you the opposite way. Aim, fire, and fly your way to the green dot, past spikes, lasers and gunships.

- **60 levels in 10 worlds.** Each world teaches one mechanic (swing, phase, teleport, gravity flip, elevators, moving spikes, pushers, lasers, gunships). The 10 finals combine three or more and run across three screens with teleporters.
- **Five difficulties,** including God Mode: one life, no mercy.
- **Built for speedrunning.** Deterministic physics at a fixed 60 Hz. Track both your fastest time and your fewest shots.
- **Race your ghost.** Your best run replays beside you.
- **Neon look, original soundtrack** by DavidKBD.
